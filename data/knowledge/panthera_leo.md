# Panthera leo

**Common Name:** Lion

**Scientific Name:** *Panthera leo*

## Conservation Status

**Vulnerable (VU)** according to the International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) Red List. Lion populations have declined over recent decades due to habitat loss, human-wildlife conflict, depletion of prey species, and illegal hunting.

## Habitat

Lions are found primarily in savannas, grasslands, open woodlands, and scrub habitats across sub-Saharan Africa. A small, isolated population also survives in the Gir Forest National Park of western India.

## Behaviour

Lions are the only truly social cats, living in family groups known as prides. A pride typically consists of related females, their cubs, and one or more adult males. Females usually cooperate when hunting, while males primarily defend the pride's territory from rival males.

## Diet

Lions are carnivorous predators that feed mainly on medium to large mammals such as zebras, wildebeest, buffalo, antelopes, and occasionally giraffes. They are opportunistic hunters and may also scavenge carcasses left by other predators.

## Ecological Importance

As apex predators, lions help regulate herbivore populations and maintain healthy ecosystem balance. Their presence is an important indicator of a functioning savanna ecosystem.

## Major Threats

- Habitat loss and fragmentation
- Human-lion conflict, particularly retaliation for livestock predation
- Declining wild prey populations
- Illegal hunting and poaching

## Interesting Fact

A lion's roar can be heard from up to 8 kilometres (5 miles) away and is used to communicate with pride members and defend territory.
